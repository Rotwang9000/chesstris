TETCHES

Tetches is a free, real-time multiplayer game that fuses chess and Tetris. Drop tetromino blocks to grow your island kingdom, then move your chess pieces across the board you built — capture a rival king and their kingdom becomes yours. Play instantly in your browser — no download, no sign-up — in one persistent shared world, or open a private Battle arena for 2–4 players.
